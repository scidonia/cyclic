# ESOP Round 1 Submission Guide (May 2026)

## Goal
Transform the paper into a **clear, defensible, high-probability ESOP acceptance** within ~3 weeks.

---

## Core Principle
> Do not improve the theory. Improve the reader experience.

---

## Target Length

- Main paper: **18–22 pages (ideal: ~20)**
- Max allowed: 25 pages (including references)
- Appendix: unlimited (use aggressively)

---

## Step-by-Step Plan

### Week 1: Positioning (Highest Impact)

#### 1. Rewrite Abstract
Must answer in 30 seconds:
- Problem: no foundation for SC in dependent types
- Insight: SC = cyclic proof search
- Result: correspondence + mechanisation
- Payoff: optimisation foundation

#### 2. Rewrite Introduction
Structure:
1. Problem
2. Why hard (dependent types)
3. Key idea
4. What it explains
5. Contributions

#### 3. Contributions Section
Use bullet points, no hype:
- calculus
- correspondence
- CIU proof
- mechanisation

#### 4. Add “Why This Matters”
Explicit:
- optimisation relevance
- dependent types challenge
- what your work enables

#### 5. Add Skeptic Defense
Answer:
“Is this just encoding?”
→ explain explanatory power (termination, recursion, correctness)

---

### Week 2: Readability Pass

#### 6. Reduce Cognitive Load
Remove:
- Coq file names
- lemma identifiers
- repetition

Add:
- guiding sentences (“At a high level…”)

#### 7. Shorten Paragraphs
Rule: one idea per paragraph

#### 8. Control Repetition
Introduce once → reuse shorthand

---

### Week 3: Polish + Submission

#### 9. Emphasize Key Insight
Highlight clearly:
> Induction is discovered via cycles

#### 10. Trim Mechanisation Section
Keep:
- “mechanised”
- “0 axioms”
Remove:
- implementation noise

#### 11. Move to Appendix
Move:
- long proofs
- detailed Coq references
- extra examples

---

## Final Checklist

- [ ] Abstract clear in 30 seconds
- [ ] Intro tells coherent story
- [ ] Contributions crisp
- [ ] “Why this matters” present
- [ ] Skeptic concern addressed
- [ ] Paragraphs short
- [ ] Noise reduced
- [ ] Tone balanced
- [ ] Double-blind compliant
- [ ] Length ~20 pages

---

## Reviewer Goal

Make reviewers think:
- “Clear”
- “Solid”
- “Correct”
- “Worth accepting”

---

## Final Rule

> Clarity > depth for Round 1.
