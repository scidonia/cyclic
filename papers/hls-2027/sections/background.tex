\section{Background: Supercompilation as Cyclic Proof Search}
\label{sec:background}

We briefly review the correspondence established in prior work, which provides
the foundation for the two extensions.

\subsection{Standard supercompilation}

A supercompiler takes a term $t$ and produces an optimised residual $t'$ such
that $t \ciu t'$ (contextual equivalence).  It operates on \emph{configurations}
--- judgements $\jTy{\Gamma}{t}{A}$ --- and builds a directed graph:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[Driving.] Apply $\beta$, $\iota$ (case/constructor), and $\delta$ (fixpoint
  unfolding) reductions.  Deterministic; produces zero or one successor.
\item[Splitting.] When the scrutinee of a \texttt{case} is a neutral variable,
  introduce one successor per constructor, extending $\Gamma$ with fresh
  constructor arguments.
\item[Folding.] When a configuration matches a previously-seen one (up to
  renaming), close the graph with a back-edge.
\item[Generalisation.] When a configuration embeds into a previous one
  (homeomorphic embedding whistle), anti-unify the two to produce a more
  general configuration that both instantiate.
\end{description}

\subsection{The mechanised pipeline}

The prior work establishes this pipeline in Rocq, with 0 axioms and 0 Admitted:
\[
  \text{SC success}
  \;\Rightarrow\;
  \text{pre-proof}
  \;\Rightarrow\;
  \texttt{trace\_condition\_ok}
  \;\Rightarrow\;
  t \ciu \text{residual}(t)
\]
The key theorem is \texttt{supercompile\_ciu\_soundness\_untyped}: for any
$\Gamma, t, A$ and any fuel,
\[
  \texttt{supercompile\_jTy\_tc}\ \mathit{fuel}\ \Sigma\ \Gamma\ t\ A = \mathsf{Some}\ (v, b)
  \;\Rightarrow\;
  t \ciu \texttt{residualise\_cfg}\ \mathit{fuel'}\ \Sigma\ b\ v\ 0\ \emptyset
\]
The hypothesis mentions only \texttt{trace\_condition\_ok} --- not
\emph{how} generalisation was performed.  This is the key property exploited
by both extensions: any generalisation strategy that produces a graph passing
the trace check inherits the CIU guarantee for free.
