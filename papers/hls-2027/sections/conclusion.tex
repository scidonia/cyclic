\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have shown that a mechanised sequent-calculus foundation for
supercompilation is more than a correctness guarantee --- it is a
\emph{design instrument}.  The precise correspondence between SC
operations and proof-theoretic concepts led to two productive extensions:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{LLM oracle for generalisation:} The identification of
  generalisation as cut introduction naturally suggests an oracle
  architecture where the kernel validates all proposals.  The oracle
  is untrusted; the CIU theorem is unconditional.  A scrambling
  experiment provides evidence of compositional reasoning.

\item \textbf{$\omega$-rule via lemma environment:} Strengthened induction
  becomes automatic through LLM-proposed auxiliary lemmas, sub-SC
  validation, and lemma-driven driving.  This enables compiler
  soundness and reverse-append distribution --- properties standard SC
  cannot prove.

\item \textbf{Dependent-type branch elimination:} Type-index comparison
  prunes impossible constructors, producing smaller residuals and
  enabling more folding opportunities.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Limitations acknowledged.}
The LLM lemma proposal succeeds on 6 programs (24 tests), including with
scrambled names.  A larger-scale evaluation remains future work.  The
type-index trace condition is implemented but does not yet enable any
theorem previously out of reach.  The CIU theorem on which the oracle
architecture depends is from companion work; this paper states the
correspondence directly.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Conditional lemmas (logical relations) would bring $\mathsf{sorted}\
(\mathsf{sort}\ l) = \mathsf{true}$ and commutativity within reach.
A full statistical evaluation across a larger corpus would strengthen
the scrambling experiment.  And the extraction bridge would make
the $\omega$-rule fully automatic.

\paragraph{Future work.}
The $\omega$-rule extension (Section~\ref{sec:limits}) is the immediate next
step: lemma environments in the driving step, validated by sub-SC runs, with
LLM-proposed auxiliary lemma statements.  This would bring $\mathsf{sorted}\ (\sort\ l) = \mathsf{true}$,
commutativity of plus, and the reversibility of permutations within reach.

Beyond HLS, the sequent perspective suggests studying SC as a decision
procedure for fragments of CIC: if the SC terminates and produces a residual
equal to a normal form, it has decided the term's observational class.  Making
this precise is a direction we plan to pursue.
