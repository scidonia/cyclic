\subsection{Cyclic proof for reverse-append fusion}
\label{sec:reverse-append-proof}

Figure~\ref{fig:reverse-append-proof} shows the cyclic proof.
The root (bottom) is $\mathsf{reverse}\;(\mathsf{append}\;\mathit{xs}\;\mathit{ys})$.
Unfolding $\mathsf{reverse}$ (\textsc{fix}) gives
$\mathsf{revAcc}\;(\mathsf{append}\;\mathit{xs}\;\mathit{ys})\;\mathsf{nil}$.
The LLM-proposed lemma (rule \textsc{lemma}) rewrites this to
$\mathsf{revAcc}\;\mathit{ys}\;(\mathsf{reverse}\;\mathit{xs})$.
Splitting on $\mathit{ys}$ ($\star$, progress event) yields a nil branch
that closes via a backlink (substitution $\mathit{ys}\mapsto\mathsf{nil}$),
and a cons branch that drives further by structural induction on $\mathit{ys}$.

\[

\RightLabel{\;\fontsize{6}{7}\selectfont\textsc{nil}}

\AxiomC{$\mathit{ys}\mapsto\mathsf{nil}$}
\RightLabel{\;\fontsize{6}{7}\selectfont\textsc{back} \textcolor{red!70}{\textdagger}}
\UnaryInfC{\textcolor{red!60}{$\mathsf{reverse}\;\mathit{xs} : \mathsf{List}$ \textcolor{red!70}{\textdagger}}}
\RightLabel{\;\fontsize{6}{7}\selectfont\textsc{cons}}

\TrinaryInfC{$\mathsf{revAcc}\;\mathit{ys}\;(\mathsf{reverse}\;\mathit{xs}) : \mathsf{List}$}

\UnaryInfC{$\mathsf{revAcc}\;(\mathsf{append}\;\mathit{xs}\;\mathit{ys})\;\mathsf{nil} : \mathsf{List}$}

\DisplayProof

\vspace{-6pt}
Without the \textsc{lemma} rule, the step from
$\mathsf{revAcc}\;(\mathsf{append}\;\mathit{xs}\;\mathit{ys})\;\mathsf{nil}$
to $\mathsf{revAcc}\;\mathit{ys}\;(\mathsf{reverse}\;\mathit{xs})$ would
fail --- the two terms do not anti-unify.  The LLM proposes the lemma as a
cut; the sub-prover proves it by induction on $\mathit{xs}$; the main proof
uses it as a rewrite.  The lemma is not trusted for soundness --- it is
validated by the proof engine's kernel checks before use.
