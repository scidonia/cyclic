\section{Proof Graph for Reverse-Append Fusion}
\label{sec:proof-graph}

Figure~\ref{fig:reverse-append-graph} shows the cyclic proof graph produced
by the lemma-driven supercompiler for the reverse-append identity
$\mathsf{reverse}\;(\mathsf{append}\;\mathit{xs}\;\mathit{ys}) =
\mathsf{append}\;(\mathsf{reverse}\;\mathit{ys})\;(\mathsf{reverse}\;\mathit{xs})$.

The standard SC gets stuck at node $v_3$: the configuration
$\mathsf{revAcc}\;(\mathsf{append}\;\mathit{xs}\;\mathit{ys})\;\mathsf{nil}$
has a recursive call $\mathsf{revAcc}\;\mathit{xs}\;(\mathsf{cons}\;\mathit{x}\;\mathsf{nil})$
that does not match the companion $\mathsf{revAcc}\;\mathit{xs}\;\mathsf{nil}$
(the accumulator differs).  Anti-unification produces a trivial generalisation
(just the function name), and no fold is possible.

The LLM proposes the lemma
$\mathsf{revAcc}\;(\mathsf{append}\;\mathit{xs}\;\mathit{ys})\;\mathit{acc}
 = \mathsf{revAcc}\;\mathit{ys}\;(\mathsf{revAcc}\;\mathit{xs}\;\mathit{acc})$
by inspecting the body of $\mathsf{revAcc}$ (a structural induction on its
list argument).  The sub-SC proves this lemma by induction on $\mathit{xs}$
(the $L$ vertex in the graph).  The lemma is added to the driving environment
as a rewrite rule.

With the lemma, the main SC at $v_3$ rewrites:
\begin{center}
$\mathsf{revAcc}\;(\mathsf{append}\;(\mathsf{cons}\;\mathit{x}\;\mathit{xs})\;\mathit{ys})\;\mathsf{nil}
 \longrightarrow \mathsf{revAcc}\;\mathit{ys}\;(\mathsf{revAcc}\;(\mathsf{cons}\;\mathit{x}\;\mathit{xs})\;\mathsf{nil})$
\end{center}
The right-hand side is now an application of $\mathsf{revAcc}\;\mathit{ys}$
to the companion $\mathsf{reverse}\;\mathit{xs}$, which is structurally
smaller in $\mathit{xs}$.  The cyclic backlink at $v_4$ closes the proof.

The graph has three distinct edge types:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[Driving edges (black):] Deterministic $\beta/\iota/\delta$ steps
  corresponding to cut-elimination in the focused sequent calculus.
\item[Lemma edges (green, dashed):] The LLM proposes a cut formula
  (lemma); the sub-SC proves it; the main SC uses it as a rewrite rule.
  This is cut \emph{introduction} --- the step that standard SC cannot
  perform.
\item[Cyclic backlink (blue):] The fold that closes the proof, with a
  progress event (the case split on $\mathit{xs}$) satisfying the
  trace condition.  The cycle structure \emph{is} the induction on
  $\mathit{xs}$.
\end{description}
